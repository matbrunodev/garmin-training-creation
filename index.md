# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 26, 2026

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the proposed application processes personal information when converting running workouts from screenshots and other user-provided formats into structured workouts for Garmin Connect.

The application is currently in the proof-of-concept stage.

## 2. Information We Process

The application may process screenshots of training plans, workout descriptions, and structured workout information provided by users.

If Garmin Connect API access is approved and implemented, the application may also process information necessary to publish user-approved workouts to Garmin Connect.

The initial version does not require access to health metrics, activity history, or other Garmin device-derived data.

## 3. Use of Artificial Intelligence

An AI service may be used to interpret user-provided screenshots and extract workout information. Any third-party AI provider used will be assessed for appropriate privacy and security safeguards.

## 4. Data Sharing

Personal information will not be sold. Data will only be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the application, subject to applicable privacy requirements and the application's actual implementation.

## 5. Data Retention and Security

The application will follow data minimization principles and implement appropriate safeguards to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. Data retention and deletion procedures will be established according to the services used and applicable legal requirements.

## 6. User Rights

Users may request information about the processing of their personal data and request applicable corrections or deletion, subject to legal obligations.
